01 Technical Mechanics

How It Works (Under the Hood)

Zenaload is architected exclusively for zero-friction top-ups. Traditional platforms require tedious registration, OTPs, and password verifications before taking payment. We bypassed the bloat by building direct server sockets to primary gaming publishers.

input
Step 01 • Instant Client-side

Guest Input & UID Validation

You simply enter your Game Player ID (UID) and choose your diamond/point bundle. Our front-end performs immediate regex pattern checks and queries live game server nodes to confirm in-game character names before you deposit a single Naira.

bolt
Step 02 • Real-Time Fintech Layer

Paystack Webhook Settlement

Choose Bank Transfer, USSD, OPay, PalmPay, or Nigerian debit cards. Once payment is authorized, Paystack hits our multi-region load-balanced webhook endpoints in sub-second latency. No manual receipt reviews or WhatsApp screen confirmation needed.

sports_esports
Step 03 • Benchmark ~24s Automated Delivery

Direct Publisher API Socket Injection

Upon webhook validation, our core transaction daemon issues an encrypted API request straight into the game publisher's regional account database. Your diamonds, UC, or CP credit directly to your game inbox in an average of 24 seconds flat.

02 Consumer Protection

100% Refund & Undelivered Policy

We adhere strictly to Nigerian consumer protection guidelines and fintech best practices. Because digital gaming assets delivered to a valid ID cannot be revoked once credited, our policies are clearly structured around delivery integrity.

timer

Automated 15-Minute Timeout Refund

If a publisher node suffers unexpected maintenance or API disconnection after your Paystack payment clears, our system enforces a hard 15-minute fulfillment threshold. If credits are not injected within 15 minutes, an automated reversal back to your original source account is triggered immediately.

badge

Wrong UID Dispute Resolution (2 Hours)

If you realize you made a typographical error in your User ID and ping our Live Support team before the API handshake begins, we pause queue dispatch. If already credited to an existing live gamer account, digital delivery is final; however, our dispute officers resolve claim inquiries within 2 hours.

receipt_long

Paystack Direct Reversals

All refunds are issued via Paystack back to the exact bank account or card originally debited. NIBSS interbank processing schedules apply (usually within 1–2 business hours for Nigerian commercial banks).

03 Zero-Trust Architecture

Privacy Policy & Zero-Password Security

Gamers are routinely vulnerable to phishing and account hijackings across Africa. Zenaload was purposefully built with a strict Zero-Password Guarantee.

lock_open_right

No Passwords Ever

We will NEVER ask for your game password, Moonton/Activision login, Facebook credentials, or Gmail access. We only require public character IDs.

credit_card_off

Zero Financial Storage

We never store card numbers, CVVs, or bank PINs on our servers. All transaction fields are hosted within Paystack's PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant secure iframes.

cookie

Local Device Cookies

Your past Game IDs and Server choices are cached strictly in your own local device storage for convenient reorders, never centralized in an accessible database.

visibility_off

Masked Receipts

Transaction references and WhatsApp order receipts mask phone numbers and email addresses to preserve privacy across shared desktop cyber-cafes.
