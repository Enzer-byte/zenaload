How fast will the Diamonds arrive in my Free Fire account? expand_more

Diamonds are dispatched automatically through our official publisher API link. In 99.4% of orders, diamonds reflect in your game inventory within 24 seconds after payment confirmation. You do not need to log out of the game.

What if I entered the wrong Player ID? expand_more

Because fulfillment is instantaneous and completely automated, top-ups executed to an existing valid UID cannot be reversed or transferred. Please verify your UID in Free Fire before finalizing the payment. If the UID is completely invalid on Garena servers, the transaction will auto-refund to your Zenaload balance.
