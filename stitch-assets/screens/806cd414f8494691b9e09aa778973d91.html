Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Zenaload tracking, processing, and delivery guarantees.

How fast is automated delivery? expand_more
Over 98% of orders are completed within 15 to 30 seconds of confirmed Paystack payment. In rare cases of publisher API maintenance, it may take up to 10 minutes.
I didn't receive my reference code. What do I do? expand_more
Use the "By Phone / Player ID" tab above to locate recent transactions associated with your gamer tag. Alternatively, check your bank debit alert description or email for your Paystack reference ID and message our support.
What if I entered the wrong Player ID or server? expand_more
If the Player ID does not exist in the publisher's system, our automated engine will reject the injection and keep your order on hold. Please message us on WhatsApp with the correct ID so we can re-trigger your delivery immediately.
My bank debited me but the order status says waiting? expand_more
Nigerian interbank transfer sessions occasionally experience 1-3 minute latency on NIBSS switches. Once Paystack confirms receipt, your status updates automatically and top-up is credited without delay.
